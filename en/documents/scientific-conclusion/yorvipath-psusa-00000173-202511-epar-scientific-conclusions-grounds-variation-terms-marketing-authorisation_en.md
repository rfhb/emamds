## Scientific conclusions

Taking into account the PRAC Assessment Report on the PSUR(s) for palopegteriparatide, the

scientific conclusions of PRAC are as follows: In view of available data on palopegteriparatide from clinical trials, EAP, spontaneous reports, including in some cases a close temporal relationship, a positive de-challenge and/or re-challenge,

the PRAC agrees with the MAH's conclusion that a causal relationship between palopegteriparatide and hypersensitivity including anaphylaxis is at least a reasonable possibility. The PRAC concluded that the product information of products containing palopegteriparatide should be amended accordingly.

Having reviewed the PRAC recommendation, the CHMP agrees with the PRAC overall conclusions and grounds for recommendation.

## Grounds for the variation to the terms of the marketing authorisation(s)

On the basis of the scientific conclusions for palopegteriparatide the CHMP is of the opinion that the benefit-risk balance of the medicinal product(s) containing palopegteriparatide is unchanged subject to the proposed changes to the product information.

The CHMP recommends that the terms of the marketing authorisation(s) should be varied.
