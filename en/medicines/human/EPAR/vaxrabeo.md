## Overview

VaxRabeo is a vaccine used to help protect people of all ages against rabies. It can be used before possible contact with rabies virus or in people who may have already been exposed to the virus.

VaxRabeo should be used in accordance with official recommendations. The active substance in VaxRabeo is the inactivated rabies virus.

### How is VaxRabeo used?

VaxRabeo is given by injection into a muscle or the skin. The vaccination schedule depends on whether the vaccine is being given before or after possible exposure to the rabies virus, whether the person has previously been vaccinated against rabies and whether the person has a weakened immune system.

Depending on the type of exposure from an animal (for example, through biting or scratching of broken skin), the person may also be given rabies antibodies (proteins that attack the virus).

The vaccine can only be obtained with a prescription. For more information about using VaxRabeo, see the package leaflet or contact your healthcare provider.

### How does VaxRabeo work?

VaxRabeo contains the rabies virus that has been inactivated and cannot cause rabies.

Vaccines work by preparing the immune system, the body's natural defences, to defend the body against a specific disease. When a person is given VaxRabeo, the immune system recognises parts of the inactivated virus as foreign and produces antibodies against them.

The antibodies produced in response to vaccination help protect against rabies by blocking the virus from attaching to cells.

### What benefits of VaxRabeo have been shown in studies?

Studies showed that adults and children vaccinated with VaxRabeo produced levels of antibodies that can provide adequate protection against rabies.

**Vaccination before possible exposure**

A main study involving 1,708 adults and children aged one year and older showed that VaxRabeo is at least as effective as two authorised rabies vaccines at triggering levels of antibodies considered protective against rabies.

Participants received vaccination according to a schedule used before exposure to the rabies virus (consisting of 2 or 3 doses). Those who received VaxRabeo had adequate levels of antibodies. Similar results were seen with the two other rabies vaccines.

**Vaccination after possible exposure**

A main study involving 640 adults showed that VaxRabeo was at least as effective as two authorised rabies vaccines at triggering levels of antibodies considered protective against rabies.

Participants had not been exposed to the rabies virus but received vaccination according to a schedule used after possible exposure (4 doses). Most also received rabies antibodies.

In this study, two weeks after the fourth dose, almost all participants who received VaxRabeo had adequate levels of antibodies. Similar results were seen with the two other rabies vaccines.

The company did not have to study the vaccine in children of all age groups as the results in the age groups studied could be reliably extrapolated to other age groups.

Studies carried out with VaxRabeo are described in more detail in the medicine's assessment report.

### What are the side effects and restrictions with VaxRabeo?

For the full list of side effects and restrictions with VaxRabeo, see the package leaflet.

The most common side effects in adults (which may affect more than 1 in 10 people) include pain at the site of the injection, muscle pain, headache and malaise (feeling unwell).

In children, the most common side effects include swelling and pain at the site of the injection. For children 12 to 23 months of age, these also include irritability and unusual crying, and for children 2 years of age and older, these include muscle pain.

### Why is VaxRabeo authorised in the EU?

Rabies is almost always fatal once symptoms develop. Preventing the disease before exposure or acting quickly after possible exposure is therefore very important.

Studies showed that VaxRabeo triggered adequate levels of antibodies against the rabies virus in adults and children. The side effects of the vaccine were mainly mild to moderate and short-lived.

The European Medicines Agency therefore decided that VaxRabeo's benefits are greater than its risks and that it can be authorised for use in the EU.

### What measures are being taken to ensure the safe and effective use of VaxRabeo?

Recommendations and precautions to be followed by healthcare professionals and patients for the safe and effective use of VaxRabeo have been included in the summary of product characteristics and the package leaflet.

As for all medicines, data on the use of VaxRabeo are continuously monitored. Suspected side effects reported with VaxRabeo are carefully evaluated and any necessary action is taken to protect patients.

VaxRabeo received a marketing authorisation valid throughout the EU on 12 November 2026.
