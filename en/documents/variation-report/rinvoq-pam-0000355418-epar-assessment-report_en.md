## 2.3. Clinical aspects

## Introduction

Upadacitinib is an oral, reversible JAK inhibitor that was approved in EU 2019 for the treatment of rheumatoid arthritis and have been approved for several other inflammatory diseases since then, including ulcerative colitis in adults (2022; EMEA/H/C/004760/X/0012/G). There is no paediatric UC indication for upadacitinib in the EU . P aediatric indications for upadacitinib include atopic dermatitis, where treatment is indicated for patients above 12 years of age, the treatment of non - segmental vitiligo for patients 12 years and older who are candidates for systemic therapy, and for the treatment of severe alopecia areata in patients 12 years and older .

This application concerns a postmarketing prospective observational study from Japan which included patients above 15 years of age with moderately to severely active UC. Efficacy and safety data from the included paediatric patients (&lt;18 years of age) are assessed below.

## Clinical study number and title

P22-962 A prospective observational study for evaluation of safety and effectiveness of Rinvoq in moderately to severely active ulcerative colitis; a Japanese post - marketing study

## Description

## Methods

This was an open - label, multicenter, prospective, central registration, non - interventional, non - comparative post - marketing surveillance conducted in accordance with Ministerial Ordinance on GPSP of the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare. Patients using upadacitinib according to the package insert were registered during the registration period based on the medical judgment of physicians and followed for safety and effectiveness for up to 60 weeks after the first dose of upadacitinib. The investigator collected data by entering data in the registration form and survey form based on the medical records in daily medical practice.

<div style=\"page-break-after: always\"></div>

## Study participants

Patients meeting all of the following criteria were included in the survey:

- ・ Patients aged 15 years or older at the start of treatment with this drug
- ・ Patients diagnosed with moderate to severe UC
- ・ Patients within 14 days after the start date of treatment with this drug (the start date of treatment with this drug is defined as Day 0)
- ・ Patients who received this drug for the first time after the approval of this drug for UC
- ・ Patients who received an explanation about participation in this survey (or their guardians if deemed necessary by the medical institution) and gave consent.

Patients who met any of the following criteria were excluded from this survey:

- ・ Patients currently participating in the study
- ・ Patients with contraindications to this drug
- ・ Patients with prior use of this drug

## Treatments

The study drug (Rinvoq) was not provided by the MAH as investigational drug; investigator used commercially available drug for this study. Dosage and administration had to be compliant with the directions described in the package insert. Patients were to be initiating Rinvoq at the investigator's discretion and independent of study enrolment. Rinvoq was administered in accordance with the terms of the local marketing authorization, and treatment of patients will be determined solely by the investigator

## Objective(s)

The primary objective of the study was to estimate incidence proportions of upadacitinib - related serious infections up to 60 weeks - long treatment period.

The secondary objectives of the study were to:

- Estimate the incidence of each adverse event (AE) listed in the Japanese upadacitinib Risk Management Plan
- Evaluate the effectiveness of upadacitinib in moderately -toseverely active UC patients by disease activity and laboratory data.

## Outcomes/endpoints

Incidence of ADRs of serious infections by Week 60 after the start of treatment with this drug.

Incidence proportions of each AE which is listed in Rinvoq J - RMP with UC patients up to 60 weeks.

- Incidence proportion of each ADR during observation period (up to 60 week)

<div style=\"page-break-after: always\"></div>

- Incidence proportion of each serious ADR (besides Serious infection) and AE during observation period (up to 60 week).
- Incidence proportion of each AE during observation period (up to 60 week)
- Incidence rate of each ADR and AE during observation period (up to 60 week)
- Incidence of herpes zoster by site (spread)

## Effectiveness endpoints:

- Proportion of patients achieving clinical remission (defined as a Partial Mayo Score ≤ 2 with no subscore &gt; 1) (Weeks 4, 8, 16, 24, 36, and 60)
- Proportion of patients achieving clinical response (defined as an improvement of ≥ 2 points and ≥ 30% from baseline in the Partial Mayo Score, plus a decrease in the rectal bleeding subscore [RBS] ≥ 1 or an absolute RBS of 0 or 1) (Weeks 4, 8, 16, 24, 36, and 60)
- Proportion of patients achieving endoscopic remission per Mayo Endoscopic Subscore (defined as a Mayo Endoscopic Subscore of 0) by Week 60 after the start of treatment with this drug
- Proportion of patients achieving endoscopic response per Mayo Endoscopic Subscore (defined as a Mayo Endoscopic Subscore of ≤ 1) by Week 60 after the start of treatment with this drug
- Change in Partial Mayo Score (mean score) from baseline (Weeks 4, 8, 16, 24, 36, and 60)
- Change in laboratory values (C - reactive protein [CRP], fecal calprotectin [F - cal], h a emoglobin [Hb], leucine - rich α2 - glycoprotein [LRG]) from baseline (Weeks 4, 8, 16, 24, 36, and 60).
- Proportion of patients achieving F - cal &lt; 150 (Weeks 4, 8, 16, 24, 36, and 60).
- Proportion of UC - related surgeries during the first 60 weeks of treatment with this drug .

## Safety results

In the safety analysis population, 5 patients (1.7%) were age &lt; 18 years. The TEAE of colitis ulcerative was observed in 1 of the 5 patients . According to the MAH, the causality assessment concluded that the TEAE was not related to upadacitinib, the event was nonserious and the outcome resolved. There were no other TEAEs, serious TEAEs, serious ADRs, or ADRs in patients &lt;18 years old.

## Discussion on clinical aspects

Study P22962 was a multicenter, prospective, specified use - results survey to confirm the safety and effectiveness of upadacitinib in patients with moderate or severe UC in clinical practice in Japan. Patients using upadacitinib according to the package insert were registered based on the medical judgment of physicians and followed for safety and effectiveness for up to 60 weeks after the first dose of upadacitinib. Overall, 308 patients 15 years or older were included in the study, whereof 5 patients were below 18 years.

There were only 2 of the 4 patients below 18 years of age in the efficacy analysis set that had an available partial Mayo score assessment at week 60, thus no conclusion could be made.

The TEAE of colitis ulcerative was observed in 1 of the 5 paediatric patients but there were no other TEAEs, serious TEAEs, serious ADRs, or ADRs in patients &lt;18 years old. The paediatric data from this study did not evoke any further concern and no update to the SmPC or RMP was deemed necessary.

## 3. CHMP overall conclusion and recommendation

Five children &lt;18 years of age were included in the study, one TEAE of colitis ulcerative was observed in one of the paediatric patients but there were no other TEAEs, serious TEAEs, serious ADRs, or ADRs in patients &lt;18 years old. The paediatric data from this study did not evoke any further concerns and no update to the SmPC or RMP was deemed necessary . The benefit - risk balance for Rinvoq remains unchanged.
