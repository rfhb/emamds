## Scientific conclusions

Taking into account the PRAC Assessment Report for the non-interventional imposed PASS final study report for the medicinal product(s) mentioned above, the scientific conclusions of CHMP are as follows:

The MAH has fulfilled the commitment in annex II to present the final results of a non-interventional post-authorisation safety study:

Study number 20150136: An Observational Study of Blinatumomab Safety and Effectiveness, Utilisation, and Treatment Practices.

The study results are in line with the results observed in registrational and other clinical trials. The study also brings data on patients with R/R ALL PH- experiencing late first relapse and upon the assessment of the results, the positive benefit risk ratio in this study population can be confirmed.

Therefore, in view of available data regarding the PASS final study report, the PRAC considered that changes to the product information and the conditions of the marketing authorisation were warranted.

The CHMP agrees with the scientific conclusions made by the PRAC.

## Grounds for the variation to the terms of the marketing authorisation(s)

On the basis of the scientific conclusions for the results of the study for the medicinal product(s) mentioned above, the CHMP is of the opinion that the benefit-risk balance of these medicinal product(s) is unchanged, subject to the proposed changes to the product information.

The CHMP is of the opinion that the terms of the marketing authorisation(s) of the medicinal product(s) mentioned above should be varied.
