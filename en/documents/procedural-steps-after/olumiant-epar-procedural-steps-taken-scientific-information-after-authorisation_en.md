Dose tapering substudy In the study BRAVE AA2, 95 patients who had received baricitinib 4 mg once daily since the initial randomisation and achieved SALT ≤ 20 at week 52 were re randomised in a double blind manner to continue 4 mg once daily or reduce dose to 2 mg once daily. Among the 47 patients who reduced the dose to 2 mg, 51 % maintained response through approximately 3 years of treatment (152 weeks) and 38 % maintained response through 4 years of treatment (200 weeks). Of the 17 patients who experienced a loss of treatment benefit after dose reduction, 12 (71%) regained clinical response upon retreatment with baricitinib 4 mg.
