## 2.3. Clinical aspects

## Introduction

The MAH submitted a final report for:

P22-963: A Study to Evaluate Safety and Effectiveness of Rinvoq in Adult Patients with Ankylosing

Spondylitis and Non-Radiographic Axial Spondyloarthritis in Real-World Japan; a Japanese post-

marketing study

## Description

This study was a non-interventional, prospective, multicenter, non-comparative study of upadacitinib in

the treatment of ankylosing spondylitis (AS) and non-radiographic axial spondyloarthritis (nr-axSpA) in

Japan. Subjects who met the inclusion criteria were centrally registered and assessed for incidence

proportion of serious infection as an adverse drug reaction, safety (other than serious infection) and

effectiveness of upadacitinib up to 52 weeks.

Methods

Data from medical records were collected in routine clinical practice using case report form (CRF).

Patients who used upadacitinib according to the package insert during the registration period at the

discretion of physicians were registered and followed up for safety and effectiveness for up to 52

weeks from the first dose.

Registration period: 26 May 2022 to 31 July 2024

Study period: 26 May 2022 to 31 December 2025

The observation period was up to 52 weeks from the start of upadacitinib treatment. If upadacitinib

treatment was discontinued or observation became impossible during the observation period, the

observation period was until this drug treatment was discontinued. The occurrence of serious Adverse

Events (AEs) up to 30 days after discontinuation of this drug was investigated.

## Study participants

A total of 104 patients were enrolled in the overall study.

The study enrolled 2 subjects under the age of 18 years of age, with a physician-confirmed diagnosis of AS or nr-axSpA, who were prescribed upadacitinib according to the label and practice in Japan.

## Results

## Efficacy results

Neither of the 2 patients aged &lt; 18 years in the effectiveness analysis population achieved BASDAI50.

## Safety results

In the safety analysis population, there were 2 patients aged &lt; 18 years, neither of whom experienced AEs or ADRs.

## Discussion on clinical aspects

The study enrolled 2 subjects under the age of 18 years of age, with a physician-confirmed diagnosis of AS or nr-axSpA, who were prescribed upadacitinib according to the label and practice in Japan. Neither of the 2 patients aged &lt; 18 years achieved BASDAI50 and neither experienced any AEs or ADRs. No updates of the SmPC or the RMP is necessary based on the findings from this study.

## 3. CHMP overall conclusion and recommendation

Study P22-963 was a non-interventional, prospective, multicenter, non-comparative study of upadacitinib in the treatment of ankylosing spondylitis (AS) and non-radiographic axial spondyloarthritis (nr-axSpA) in Japan. Subjects who met the inclusion criteria were centrally registered and assessed for incidence proportion of serious infection as an adverse drug reaction, safety (other than serious infection) and effectiveness of upadacitinib up to 52 weeks. The overall study enrolled 104 subjects.

The study enrolled 2 subjects under the age of 18 years of age, with a physician-confirmed diagnosis of AS or nr-axSpA, who were prescribed upadacitinib according to the label and practice in Japan. Neither of the 2 patients aged &lt; 18 years achieved BASDAI50 and neither experienced any AEs or ADRs.

No updates of the SmPC or the RMP is necessary based on the findings from this study.
