To update section 4.4 of the SmPC in order to add information regarding cases of Crohn's disease; a warning concerning Crohn's disease cases reported in NiemannPick type C (NP-C) disease patients treated with miglustat in post-marketing setting has been reported. In addition, PRAC considered that the PI should be updated in order to better highlight the risk of reduced growth in the paediatric population. In addition the MAH has taken this opportunity to make minor corrections in the description of the packaging and of the capsules.
