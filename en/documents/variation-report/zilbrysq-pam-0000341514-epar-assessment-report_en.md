## Efficacy results

Exploratory objective: describe changes in MG-ADL score(s) following the initiation of zilucoplan Results were limited to 9 patients who had more than 1 MG-ADL score across follow-up. 5 experienced a mean reduction of 1.2 points (SD: 2.59) compared to baseline and 2 out of 5 patients (40%) achieved a &gt;2-point reduction in MG-ADL score.

## Safety results

No safety data gathered

## Discussion on clinical aspects

In this observational analysis of patients initiating zilucoplan, demographic and disease characteristics were broadly comparable to the phase 3 RAISE population, although patients in routine practice were older (mean age 60.66 years vs 52.60 years in RAISE) and demonstrated slightly lower baseline MGADL scores (8.16 vs 10.30), suggesting inclusion of a broader and potentially less severe disease spectrum. Most patients (70.01%) initiated the highest approved dose with strong weight-dose concordance (94.54%), and the majority were receiving concomitant MG therapies at initiation, indicating use within established treatment regimens. Vaccination uptake was high (93.19% for both MenACWY and MenB).

## 3. CHMP overall conclusion and recommendation

This was a retrospective longitudinal observational study of adult patients with gMG who were enrolled into zilucoplan PSP and/or SP systems in the USA. In the US, individuals ≥17 years of age are included in adult indications. No individual efficacy data and no safety data are presented in the provided study report. Thus, no conclusion on efficacy or safety in paediatric patients can be drawn from the study.
