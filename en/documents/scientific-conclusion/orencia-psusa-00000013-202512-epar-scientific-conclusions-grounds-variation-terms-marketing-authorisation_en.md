## Scientific conclusions

Taking into account the PRAC Assessment Report on the PSUR(s) for abatacept, the scientific conclusions of PRAC are as follows:

As part of the RMP update approved in this PSUSA procedure, the information included in the additional risk minimisation measure, the Patient Card, has been updated. The PRAC concluded that the product information of products containing abatacept should be amended accordingly.

Having reviewed the PRAC recommendation, the CHMP agrees with the PRAC overall conclusions and grounds for recommendation.

## Grounds for the variation to the terms of the marketing authorisation(s)

On the basis of the scientific conclusions for abatacept the CHMP is of the opinion that the benefit-risk balance of the medicinal product(s) containing abatacept is unchanged subject to the proposed changes to the product information

The CHMP recommends that the terms of the marketing authorisation(s) should be varied.
