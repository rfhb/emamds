## Overview

Ruxolitinib Zentiva is a medicine used to treat the following conditions:

- splenomegaly (enlarged spleen) or other disease-related symptoms such as fever, night sweats, bone pain and weight loss in adults who have myelofibrosis. Myelofibrosis is a disease in which the bone marrow becomes very dense and rigid and produces abnormal, immature blood cells;
- polycythaemia vera in adults in whom the medicine hydroxycarbamide (also known as hydroxyurea) does not work or causes unacceptable side effects. In polycythaemia vera, too many red blood cells are produced, which can cause reduced blood flow to the organs due to 'thickening' of the blood and occasionally the formation of blood clots;
- acute graft-versus-host disease (GvHD, when donor cells attack the body shortly after a transplant) in adults and children aged 28 days and older for whom corticosteroids or other systemic therapies (treatments given by mouth or injection) did not work well enough;
- chronic graft-versus-host-disease (which usually develops later than acute GvHD, within several weeks to months after a transplant) in adults and children aged 6 months and older for whom corticosteroids or other systemic therapies did not work well enough.

Ruxolitinib Zentiva contains the active substance ruxolitinib and is a 'generic medicine'. This means that Ruxolitinib Zentiva contains the same active substance and works in the same way as a 'reference medicine' already authorised in the EU. The reference medicine for Ruxolitinib Zentiva is Jakavi. For more information on generic medicines, see the question-and-answer document [here](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/other/questions-and-answers-generic-medicines_en.pdf).

### How is Ruxolitinib Zentiva used?

Ruxolitinib Zentiva can only be obtained with a prescription and treatment should only be started by a doctor who is experienced with the use of cancer medicines.

Ruxolitinib Zentiva is available as tablets to be taken by mouth twice daily. The recommended dose depends on the condition for which it is used.

Ruxolitinib Zentiva tablets are not suitable for patients who cannot swallow tablets whole and children less than 6 years old. Other medicines containing ruxolitinib are available for these patients.

The doctor should reduce the dose or stop treatment if certain side effects occur.

For more information about using Ruxolitinib Zentiva, see the package leaflet or contact your doctor or pharmacist.

### How does Ruxolitinib Zentiva work?

The active substance in Ruxolitinib Zentiva, ruxolitinib, works by blocking a group of enzymes known as Janus kinases (JAKs), which are involved in the production and growth of blood cells.

In myelofibrosis and polycythaemia vera, there is too much JAK activity, leading to the abnormal production of blood cells. These blood cells can build up in organs, including the spleen, causing them to become enlarged.

JAKs are also involved in the development and activation of cells of the immune system (the body's natural defences) that play a role in GvHD. By blocking JAKs, ruxolitinib helps to reduce inflammation, thereby reducing the symptoms of acute and chronic GvHD.

### How has Ruxolitinib Zentiva been studied?

Studies on the benefits and risks of the active substance in the authorised uses have already been carried out with the reference medicine, Jakavi, and do not need to be repeated for Ruxolitinib Zentiva.

As for every medicine, the company provided studies on the quality of Ruxolitinib Zentiva. The company also carried out a study that showed that it is 'bioequivalent' to the reference medicine. Two medicines are bioequivalent when they produce the same levels of the active substance in the body and are therefore expected to have the same effect.

Studies carried out with Ruxolitinib Zentiva are described in more detail in the medicine's assessment report.

### What are the benefits and risks of Ruxolitinib Zentiva?

Because Ruxolitinib Zentiva is a generic medicine and is bioequivalent to the reference medicine, its benefits and risks are taken as being the same as the reference medicine's.

### Why is Ruxolitinib Zentiva authorised in the EU?

The European Medicines Agency concluded that, in accordance with EU requirements, Ruxolitinib Zentiva has been shown to have comparable quality and to be bioequivalent to Jakavi. Therefore, the Agency's view was that, as for Jakavi, the benefits of Ruxolitinib Zentiva outweigh the identified risks and it can be authorised for use in the EU.

### What measures are being taken to ensure the safe and effective use of Ruxolitinib Zentiva?

Recommendations and precautions to be followed by healthcare professionals and patients for the safe and effective use of Ruxolitinib Zentiva have been included in the summary of product characteristics and the package leaflet. Any additional measures in place for Jakavi also apply to Ruxolitinib Zentiva where appropriate.

As for all medicines, data on the use of Ruxolitinib Zentiva are continuously monitored. Suspected side effects reported with Ruxolitinib Zentiva are carefully evaluated and any necessary action taken to protect patients.

### Other information about Ruxolitinib Zentiva

Ruxolitinib Zentiva received a marketing authorisation valid throughout the EU on 12 November 2026.

### Therapeutic indication

**Myelofibrosis (MF)**

Ruxolitinib Zentiva is indicated for the treatment of disease-related splenomegaly or symptoms in adult patients with primary myelofibrosis (also known as chronic idiopathic myelofibrosis), post polycythaemia vera myelofibrosis or post essential thrombocythaemia myelofibrosis.

**Polycythaemia vera (PV)**

Ruxolitinib Zentiva is indicated for the treatment of adult patients with polycythaemia vera who are resistant to or intolerant of hydroxyurea.

**Graft versus host disease (GvHD)**

*Acute GvHD*

Ruxolitinib Zentiva is indicated for the treatment of adults and paediatric patients aged 28 days and older with acute graft versus host disease who have inadequate response to corticosteroids or other systemic therapies (see section 5.1).

*Chronic GvHD*

Ruxolitinib Zentiva is indicated for the treatment of adults and paediatric patients aged 6 months and older with chronic graft versus host disease who have inadequate response to corticosteroids or other systemic therapies (see section 5.1).
